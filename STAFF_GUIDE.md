# Farm’s Touch: staff guide

Open **your-shop-address/admin** on your phone and log in with your **email or mobile number and the password the owner gave you**. Change it any time with **Change password**.

## Packer

1. Open **Packing** and choose the delivery day (usually **Tomorrow**).
2. **Pick list**: the total packs of each item for that day. Print it, collect everything from the store and tick each row.
3. Tap an order card. Tick every item as it goes into the bag. Ticking the first item starts packing, and the customer sees “Packing”.
4. If an item is missing, check the order: it says **“Ask before replacing”** or **“Remove if unavailable”**. Tap **Change items / unavailable**, lower the quantity or tick the missing basket item, write the reason and tap **Save & tell customer**. Stock and the bill update automatically, and the customer gets a WhatsApp message with the new total.
5. When every item is ticked, tap **Mark packed**. Tap **Slip** to print a packing slip for the bag.
6. **Products**: after counting the shelf, type the real stock and tap **Save**. Customers can’t order more than this.

## Sending WhatsApp updates

After you tap a button such as Confirm, Change items, Start delivery or Delivered, a **“Tell the customer”** box may appear. Tap **Send on WhatsApp**: WhatsApp opens with the message ready, so just press **Send**. If the shop has set up automatic WhatsApp, this box doesn’t appear because messages go out on their own.

## Delivery partner

1. **My deliveries** lists only your orders, with the address and the amount to collect.
2. Tap **Start delivery** when you leave the store. The customer sees “Out for delivery”, your name and a Call button.
3. Use **Call**, **WhatsApp** or **Map** to reach the customer.
4. At the door, collect the money (cash or UPI), then tap **Delivered**, type the amount you collected and confirm.
5. If you can’t deliver (customer not reachable, gate closed), tap **Couldn’t deliver** and write the reason. The order goes back to the store.
6. **Delivered today** shows the cash you need to hand over.

## Admin (owner / manager)

- **Dashboard**: today’s orders and sales, the pipeline, top items, low stock, cash per delivery partner, and a CSV export for Excel.
- **Orders**: confirm new orders (tick several, then **Confirm selected**), search by order ID, name or phone, and open an order to see everything and its history.
- **Dispatch**: choose a delivery partner for each packed order, then tap **Send out**.
- **Products**: set prices, MRP and stock, hide or show items, and add new products.
- **Staff**: add people with their sign-in email and a role (Admin, Packer or Delivery). Untick **Active** to remove access.
- **WhatsApp**: messages waiting to be sent (one tap each), failed messages with the reason, an offer sender, and the customer list with order-update and offer switches (turn them off if someone replies STOP).
- **Cancel**: customers can cancel until packing starts. Admins can cancel any order before delivery, and its stock goes back automatically. The customer is told on WhatsApp.
- **Staff**: forgotten password? Edit the person, type a new password and save. They are logged out everywhere and can log in with the new one.
